Pokédex Tracker now covers every generation, so you can collect the full National Dex.

## New
- **All 9 generations (#1–1025)**: an **All** tab plus Gen 1–9 tabs in a scrolling row. Existing installs download the new Pokémon automatically on next launch.
- **National Dex progress**: a "37 / 1025 CAUGHT" counter with a percentage. Each generation tab shows its own count and gets a ✓ when it's complete.
- **Missing filter**: switch between All, Missing and Caught. Missing is your to-catch list.
- **Search**: find a Pokémon by name or number from the grid. It ignores accents and punctuation, so "mr mime" and "flabebe" both work.
- **Names on tiles**: every grid tile now shows the Pokémon's name as well as its number.
- **Backup & restore**: export your collection to a JSON file and restore it on any phone (⋮ menu). Restoring merges and never deletes anything.
- **Regional forms setting**: Alolan, Galarian, Hisuian and Paldean cards are now treated the same way. They're hidden by default; turn on ⋮ → Count regional forms to include them.
- **Faster adding**: tap a card to add it instantly, with **Undo**, and stay on the screen to add more. Cards with several variants open a picker instead.
- **Variants, quantity, condition and language**: log Holo, Reverse Holo, 1st Edition and Unlimited prints separately, own several copies, and record each copy's condition (Cardmarket scale) and language. Your last choices are remembered.
- **Card grid on the Pokédex page**: your cards for a Pokémon are now a grid showing copy counts. Tap one to change quantities, add a copy or remove it.
- **Value tab**: a quiet tab at the bottom shows your collection's total worth and the cards worth more than €3, using Cardmarket trend prices.
- **Offline mode**: save the whole Pokédex (pages, sprites and your card art) for offline use from the ⋮ menu. The app shows when you're offline and retries by itself when you're back.
- **Gotcha!**: your first card of a Pokémon gets a catch animation and a haptic buzz, and completing a generation gets its own banner.

Cards you logged in v1.0 carry over as one near-mint English copy with the variant not set.

## Fixed
- The grid no longer jumps back to #001 when you return from a Pokémon's page.
- Android's automatic backup now includes the latest changes to your collection and your settings.
- The Pokédex header folds away while you scroll the grid, leaving more room for Pokémon.

## Install
Download `pokedex-tracker-v1.1.0.apk` below and open it on an Android 8.0+ phone.

If you have v1.0.0 installed: it was signed with a temporary key, so Android may refuse to update it in place. In that case, uninstall v1.0.0 first. Uninstalling removes cards you logged in it, and v1.0.0 has no backup option, so note them down before you uninstall. From v1.1.0 on, use ⋮ → Back up collection before any reinstall.

Uses the free, keyless [PokeAPI](https://pokeapi.co) and [Pokémon TCG API](https://pokemontcg.io).
