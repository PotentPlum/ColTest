Fill a whole set in a few taps, and keep a list of the cards you're still looking for.

## New
- **Sets tab with Quick add.** Browse every set, newest first, with its logo and how many of its cards you own. Search by name or code (e.g. "30C") and filter to sets you've started or completed. Open a set to see all its cards in number order:
  - **One tap adds one copy.** Owned cards get a **− n +** button for more copies or to remove one. Every tap shows "+1" and offers Undo.
  - The **Adding as** bar sets the condition and language every tap uses.
  - A card that only comes as a normal and a holo version gets the normal one. Cards with more versions (1st Edition, Unlimited…) open a quick chooser.
  - **Trainer and Energy cards are listed too.** They count toward the set, but not toward your Pokédex.
  - Hold a card for the full card panel. An All / Missing / Owned filter turns the set into a checklist.
- **Wanted tab.** Mark cards with **Want this card** in the card panel and they show up here with their Cardmarket price and the total.
  - Cards that would add a **new Pokémon** to your Pokédex come first, marked NEW CATCH.
  - Sort by price, Pokédex number, set or date added.
  - **Got it** adds a copy and takes the card off the list. Swipe a card away to remove it.
  - Wanted cards get a ★, also on their Pokémon in the Pokédex grid.
  - Your wanted list is included in backups.

## Install
Download `pokedex-tracker-v1.3.0.apk` below and open it on an Android 8.0+ phone.

**Updating from v1.2.0:** this build is signed with a different temporary key, so Android will probably refuse to install it over v1.2.0. To keep your collection:
1. In v1.2.0, open ⋮ → **Back up collection** and save the file.
2. Uninstall v1.2.0, then install v1.3.0.
3. Open ⋮ → **Restore from backup** and pick the file.

Uses the free, keyless [PokeAPI](https://pokeapi.co) and [Pokémon TCG API](https://pokemontcg.io).
