First release of Pokédex Tracker: track your Pokémon TCG collection against the Gen 1–3 National Pokédex.

## Features
- **Generation Grid**: Gen 1, 2 and 3 tabs with a "caught" counter. Pokémon you have no cards for are gray; collected ones are in color with a badge showing how many unique card variants you've logged.
- **Pokédex Detail**: name, Dex #, types, height, weight and flavor text from PokeAPI, plus a scrolling gallery of the cards you own. Tap a card to zoom in or remove it.
- **Add Card**: lists every valid card for that Pokémon as soon as it opens. Filter by set, sort by year, or search by set, number or rarity, then tap and confirm to add.
- **Strict rules engine**: Trainer and Energy cards, TAG TEAM cards, and Alolan/Galarian cards are hidden from search results.
- **Offline**: the Pokédex list, detail pages and card art are cached on the device after first use.

## Install
Download `pokedex-tracker-v1.0.0.apk` below and open it on an Android 8.0+ phone. You'll need to allow installs from unknown sources.

Uses the free, keyless [PokeAPI](https://pokeapi.co) and [Pokémon TCG API](https://pokemontcg.io).
